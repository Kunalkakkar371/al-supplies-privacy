Effective 13 November 2026

1. Who is responsible

The "A&L Supplies Inventory Integration" (the "integration") is run by A&L Supplies, 7080 W. 300 N., Anderson, IN 46011, USA ("we", "us"), an eBay seller. It is a private tool used only for our own eBay account. It is not offered to the public, and it does not access any other eBay member's account. We have engaged a technical contractor to build and operate its technical parts on our behalf; the contractor handles the data only for the purposes in this policy.

Contact: lorenrinehart@gmail.com

2. What this policy covers, and its status

This policy describes the integration in its planned read-only phase: it will read our own eBay listing information so we can set up our Shopify store. It does not change eBay listings or inventory, and it does not synchronize quantities between eBay and Shopify. Before we turn on inventory synchronization or any other feature that writes to eBay, we will update this policy.

Status as of 13 November 2026: set-up. We have not yet retrieved any listing data from eBay.

  • Live now: the service that receives eBay's account-deletion notifications. It checks eBay's digital signature and stores a minimal record (section 5) before acknowledging each notice.
  • Not live yet: an immediate alert to our team when a notice concerns the seller; the scheduled cleanup that limits notification records to 12 months; the cleanup routine for retrieved files; and the named person responsible for handling notices. We will not retrieve any data from eBay until these are in place, and we will update this page when they are.

3. What we access on eBay

Only after the seller approves access on eBay's own authorization screen, and only to read, we will retrieve information about our own listings:

  • item number, title, description, photos, category, price and available quantity;
  • condition and condition notes, and item specifics (such as brand and model);
  • our custom label (SKU) and product identifiers such as UPC;
  • vehicle compatibility data, where a listing has it;
  • the seller's eBay user ID, which appears on the seller's own listings.

We do not access buyers' personal information, orders, messages, payment details, shipping addresses or feedback, and we never see the seller's eBay password. The extraction tool is restricted to read-only requests.

4. Why we use it

We copy our own listing information into our Shopify store so the same products can be offered there. We use the data for no other purpose. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

5. How the data is stored and who can reach it

  • Access. Access to the data and credentials is limited to authorized project personnel.
  • Credentials. The integration's eBay application secret and the seller's authorization token will be kept in the operating computer's credential store (the macOS Keychain). Short-lived access tokens will be held only in memory while a request runs.
  • Listing data. Retrieved listing data and photos will be saved in a private folder on the operating computer, readable only by the operator's user account, until they are loaded into Shopify. Once imported, they are held in our Shopify store under Shopify's terms.
  • Notification service. A small service hosted on Cloudflare receives eBay's account-deletion notifications (section 7). It stores only the notification ID, dates and a shortened one-way hash of the user ID. It does not store user IDs or usernames in readable form.
  • All connections to eBay, Shopify and Cloudflare use HTTPS.

7. eBay account-deletion notifications and deletion requests

We subscribe to eBay's Marketplace Account Deletion notifications. When one arrives, our service checks eBay's digital signature and records the minimal hashed entry described above. We hold no data about buyers or other eBay members, so a notice about another member requires nothing beyond that record.

Before we retrieve any seller data, we will add an immediate alert to our team for any notice that concerns the seller, and a named person on our team will be responsible for acting on it. We will then delete the seller's retrieved files and the authorization token that we hold, and review the imported records in Shopify. This deletion will be carried out by people, by hand; it is not automated.

To ask what data we hold, or to ask us to delete it, write to lorenrinehart@gmail.com. We handle requests as quickly as we reasonably can.
